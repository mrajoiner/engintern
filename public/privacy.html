Resumes stay in your browser.

Resume text, detected skills, saved jobs, and application notes are stored on this device using local storage and IndexedDB when you use those features. Our application does not upload them to a server, an AI service, or an employer. They remain until you remove them or clear your browser data. Avoid saving a resume on a shared device.

No outside font calls. No added ad trackers.

Fonts, map files, and resume-reading libraries are served by this site. Our application does not include advertising trackers, third-party analytics, or marketing cookies. Browser storage supports the resume, save, and tracking features you choose to use.

Opening the site still uses hosting.

The hosting provider processes technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and secure the site. Browser-only resume storage does not eliminate that hosting processing. Provider retention and international-transfer arrangements require separate operator review.

You choose when to leave.

Job feeds are retrieved by our server. Employer links open a separate website with its own privacy policy and application form. Your resume is not sent just by clicking a job link. We send no referrer header with links from this application.

Clear what you saved here.

The button below removes this site's saved resume, skills, jobs, and application notes from this browser. It does not remove applications you submitted directly to employers or data on another device.
